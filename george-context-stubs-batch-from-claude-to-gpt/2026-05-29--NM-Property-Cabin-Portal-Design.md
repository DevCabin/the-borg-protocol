## Context

George owns land in New Mexico and is planning to build a cabin there — part of a longer-term relocation vision away from the humid climate of Gilmer, TX. Multiple sessions covered climate research, cabin kit sourcing, a portal/ramada structure (designed to be built first), and a full solo build sequence.

## Key Decisions Made

- **Build-first strategy:** Build the open-air portal/ramada structure before the cabin. It serves as covered workspace, staging area for cabin materials, outdoor living/dining area, and RV shelter during the cabin build.
- **Portal design:** Hybrid of square PT posts + doubled header beams (not round/masonry columns) + round vigas as cross-rafters (authentic NM character) + latilla fill between vigas + corrugated metal top.
- **Columns:** 6×6 pressure-treated square posts in concrete footings with Simpson post bases — NOT the brick/masonry columns in the reference RV shelter photo.
- **Cabin style:** Nordic prefab-style with interlocking plank walls (Allwood-style Swedish construction). BZB Cabins and Whole Wood Cabins also evaluated.
- **Cabin sequencing:** Portal up first → use as staging area → build cabin second.
- **Solo build:** Estimated 6–8 weeks at weekends-and-evenings pace, with ~5–7 helper days for specific phases (corner posts, upper wall courses, top plate/purlin beam, roof framing, metal roofing panels).
- **Climate preference confirmed:** Drier climate strongly preferred. NM high desert suits George's stated desire to escape East Texas humidity.
- **Relocation research:** Deming NM, Alamogordo NM, Roswell NM, Colorado Springs CO, Las Vegas NV evaluated vs. Gilmer TX on humidity, temps, extreme weather.

## Important Details

- **Cost estimates (hybrid style, materials only):**
  - RV/Carport (20×24): ~$2,400–2,700 materials / ~$3,500–4,500 all-in
  - Courtyard (16×20): ~$1,800–2,100 materials / ~$2,600–3,400 all-in
  - Shade Portal (12×16): ~$1,200–1,500 materials / ~$1,700–2,400 all-in
- **NM construction notes:** Use ground-contact rated PT posts; consider 3 ft depth below grade for frost line.
- **Viga suppliers:** Verify locally in Albuquerque/Taos area (e.g., VigasNM) for larger order pricing.
- **PT lumber price reference:** ~2025 Lowe's/HD market rates used — verify locally before purchasing.
- **Build sequence helper moments (from `cabin_build_sequence.md`):**
  - Corner posts: 1 helper, 2–4 hours
  - Upper wall courses + headers: 1 helper, 1–2 days
  - Top plate + purlin beam: 1–2 helpers, 1 day
  - Roof framing + sheathing: 1 helper, 1–2 days
  - Metal roofing panels: 1 helper, 1 day
- **Cabin kit reference:** Allwood (allwoodoutlet.com) — Swedish, Nordic spruce, interlocking plank walls, insulation optional add-on.
