## Context

Multiple sessions built out ScamBomb's full Facebook marketing operation — from initial campaign structure confusion through audience strategy, ad copy production, content calendar planning, and the first month of posts. George has a 67-year-old helper named Jeff who handles posting, so all content must be copy-paste forward.

## Key Decisions Made

- **Campaign type:** Leads objective (not boost), "Website" conversion location (not Instant Forms — locked after first save, cannot be changed; fix is new ad set).
- **Audience:** Ages 45–65, United States. AARP Medicare as detailed interest. Advantage+ dismissed in favor of Manual for full targeting control.
- **Primary demographic targeted first:** Seniors (55–65+). Caregiver demo (30–45) is Phase 2.
- **Personas:** 62-year-old grandfather; 55-year-old grandmother — aware of scams, not tech-savvy, genuinely concerned for family safety.
- **Content angles:** Protective ("don't let this happen to your family"), High Dollar ("one text cost them $40,000"), Shame-removal ("he was too embarrassed to ask"), Urgency ("don't get caught off guard").
- **Ad video:** Square format (1:1), `SQ 16bil FB ad.mp4`. Headline: "Is That Text a Scam? Know in 3 Seconds." CTA: "Sign up."
- **All stats must be sourced before use** — fabricated stats or stories are FTC compliance risks. Use real sourced cases (Sharon Brightwell, Dover FL; Charles Lafkoff, Houston TX).
- **Native Facebook Lead Forms** adopted over landing page forms (avoids GHL form ID mismatch issues in workflow triggers).
- **Comment-reply flows** are the cold traffic workaround since GHL cannot initiate Facebook cold DMs.
- **Jeff handles posting** via GHL Social Planner. Posts must be pre-scheduled with explicit step-by-step GHL instructions.
- **Post frequency:** 3x/week (Mon/Wed/Fri at 10 AM CT).

## Important Details

- **Facebook Ads Manager hierarchy:** Campaign → Ad Set → Ad. Draft ads cannot be duplicated (grayed out) until published — publish the draft first with campaign toggled off to prevent going live.
- **Instagram Explore Home placement warning:** Square and landscape videos generate dimension warnings; non-blocking, safe to ignore.
- **YouTube URLs cannot be used** as Facebook ad video sources — must upload MP4 directly.
- **Pixel warning ("hasn't fired in 7 days")** at publish is non-blocking.
- **Broken original ad set** ("Protect Parents 1" with Instant Forms / no ads) remains in account — should be deleted on next login.
- **Source bank maintained** in strategy document for real victim stories and FBI/FTC statistics.
- **Key sourced stats:**
  - Seniors lost $4.8B in 2024 (FBI IC3, 43% increase over 2023)
  - Average 60+ victim loss: $83,000
  - 1 in 4 adults encountered AI voice cloning scam (McAfee)
  - 77% of those lost money ($500–$15,000 range)
